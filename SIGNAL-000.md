PURPOSE OF THIS RECORD

This document establishes the initial public record of SIGNAL 01.

The purpose is to document the existence of the project and its experimental protocol before any alleged response is interpreted as evidence of unknown intelligence.

⸻

INITIAL QUESTION

The project asks:

Can an unknown intelligence provide information that was not available to the investigators before the response and that can subsequently be verified independently?

No assumption is made that such an intelligence exists.

⸻

HYPOTHESES

SIGNAL 01 allows several possible explanations:

* extraterrestrial intelligence;
* unknown artificial intelligence;
* hypothetical future intelligence;
* natural phenomenon;
* human activity;
* technical artifact;
* coincidence;
* error or misinterpretation.

No hypothesis is considered established in advance.

PRE-REGISTRATION PRINCIPLE

The criteria for evaluating possible responses are established before interpreting them.

The project will not modify its criteria solely to accommodate an interesting result.

If the methodology changes, the change will be documented as a new version.

⸻

RESPONSE IDENTIFICATION

Any potentially significant event will receive a sequential identifier:

SIGNAL-001

SIGNAL-002

SIGNAL-003

and so forth.

The original information associated with each event should be preserved.

⸻

IMPORTANT LIMITATION

This record does not constitute evidence of:

* extraterrestrial intelligence;
* artificial intelligence from the future;
* paranormal communication;
* communication through consciousness;
* or any other extraordinary phenomenon.

It establishes only that the experiment and its criteria existed at the time of registration.
