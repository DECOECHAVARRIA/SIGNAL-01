The question

What if an unknown intelligence were capable of receiving a structured message?

SIGNAL 01 does not assume that such an intelligence exists.

We investigate the possibility openly, while maintaining alternative explanations such as coincidence, natural phenomena, human activity, technical artifacts, or error.

The central question is:

Can a possible response provide new information that can be independently verified?

Evidence first

A coincidence is not automatically a signal.

A dream is not automatically a message.

An unusual pattern is not automatically evidence.

A possible response must be documented and tested independently.

Before interpreting any result, we will record:

* what was observed;
* when it was observed;
* what information was already publicly available;
* what would count as confirmation;
* what would count as failure;
* alternative explanations.

⸻

Possible future intelligence hypothesis

One hypothesis considered by this project is that an extremely advanced artificial intelligence associated with a future civilization could theoretically possess capabilities we do not currently understand.

This is a speculative hypothesis, not an established fact.

It will never be accepted merely because an event appears mysterious.

Extraordinary claims require independently verifiable evidence.

⸻

Dream and consciousness reports

Reports involving dreams, meditation, intuition or altered states may be documented.

However, subjective experiences alone are not considered evidence.

A report becomes scientifically interesting only if it contains specific information that was recorded before verification and can subsequently be tested independently.
